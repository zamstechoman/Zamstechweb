Section 01

Executive Summary

In the telecommunications industry, a Greenfield Network refers to a network built completely from scratch without relying on legacy infrastructure, vintage database schemas, or legacy hardware constraints. When Vodafone Oman launched as the Sultanate's third mobile operator, it was established purely as a greenfield deployment.

This research paper, led by Mr. Seyed Ussain Iqbal (Founder, Amity Node) for technical, networking, and data analysis support, together with field data curated with Mr. Sajad (Sales Executive, Vodafone Oman), analyzes the operational agility and empirical radio frequency (RF) benchmarks comparing Vodafone Oman, Ooredoo Oman, and Omantel (via an Indian Airtel roaming connection).

Section 05

Architectural Comparison: Greenfield vs Legacy Hybrid

Comparing Vodafone Oman's cloud-native deployment with legacy operator structures.

Vodafone Greenfield Cloud Architecture

Cloud-Native
  • Zero Legacy Overhead: Completely free from maintenance of legacy 2G/3G hardware.
  • 100% Digital Stack: API-first provisioning, instant eSIM, and cloud database agility.
  • 5G SA Native: Built for high throughput and ultra-low latency microservices.

Incumbent Legacy Hybrid Architecture

Multi-Layer
  • High Maintenance: Substantial operational cost maintaining legacy 2G/3G hardware layers.
  • Complex Integration: Interoperability requirements between legacy BSS/OSS stacks and 5G NSA.
  • Deep Physical Reach: Extensive nationwide coverage built over decades of regional presence.

Section 06

Real-World Application Benchmarks

Observed service behaviors based on measured radio parameters.

Ultra HD Video & Low-Latency Gaming

With an SNR of 22.6 dB and RSRP of -84 dBm, Vodafone Oman provides minimal bit-error rates (BER), maintaining high 256QAM modulation order without constellation degradation.

Standard Web & Enterprise Applications

Ooredoo’s connection at -89 dBm RSRP and 8.6 dB SNR reliably supports web browsing and SaaS sessions, though higher ambient noise can cause minor latency variances during peak indoor hours.

International Inbound Roaming

The Airtel SIM roaming on Omantel registers 5G SA capability. However, user-plane traffic routing back to the home PLMN gateway via international GTP signaling adds round-trip latency overhead.

Section 07

Key Research Conclusions

1. Greenfield Operational Efficiency: Vodafone Oman’s greenfield cloud-native model allows rapid service deployment, smaller physical footprint, and excellent RF performance in urban centers.

2. Incumbent Geographical Reach: Omantel and Ooredoo leverage decades of physical capital investment to provide extensive regional coverage across Oman’s mountains, wadis, and highways.

3. Spectrum Strategy: Band B3 (1800 MHz) with 20 MHz bandwidth serves as the key capacity anchor across all three operators in urban sectors.
